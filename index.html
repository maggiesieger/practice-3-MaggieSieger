Piet Mondrian

A painter from Dutch who spent man years making paintings by removing images until the only thing that remained were straight lines and three colors.

This painting from 1930, is Composition with Red, Blue, and Yellow. Made with oil on canvas, marking 46x46 centimeters.

1872   Born in Amersfoort, the Netehrlands

1911   Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917   Co-founds De Stijl with Theo van Doesburg

1930   Paints Composition with Red, Blue, and Yellow

1944   Dies in New York
